## Overview
- PMMVY is a maternity benefit scheme of the Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD), implemented with effect from 1 January 2017.
- It is implemented under Section 4 of the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013.
- Under Mission Shakti (applicable from 1 April 2022), PMMVY is part of the "Samarthya" sub-scheme. This version is called PMMVY 2.0.
- Objectives:
  - Cash incentive as partial compensation for wage loss, so the woman can take adequate rest before and after delivery of the first child.
  - Improve health-seeking behaviour among Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM).
  - Promote positive behaviour towards the girl child by giving an additional cash incentive for the second child, if that child is a girl.
- Money is paid directly to the beneficiary's own Bank or Post Office account by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Eligibility
- Benefit is for the first two living children, provided the second child is a girl.
- The woman must belong to a socially and economically disadvantaged section. The guideline lists these categories:
  - Women belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.
  - Women who are partially (40%) or fully disabled (Divyang Jan).
  - Women holding a BPL ration card.
  - Women beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY) under Ayushman Bharat.
  - Women holding an e-Shram card.
  - Women farmers who are beneficiaries under Kisan Samman Nidhi.
  - Women holding an MGNREGA job card.
  - Women whose net family income is less than ₹8 lakh per annum.
  - Pregnant and lactating Anganwadi Workers (AWW), Anganwadi Helpers (AWH) and ASHAs.
  - Any other category prescribed by the Central Government.
- Not eligible: women in regular employment with the Central Government, State Government or a Public Sector Undertaking, or women who receive similar benefits under any other law in force.
- For the second child (girl), registration during pregnancy is mandatory.
- If a woman delivers twins, triplets or quadruplets in her second pregnancy and one or more of the children is a girl, she receives the incentive for a second girl child.
- Miscarriage or still birth: the woman is treated as a fresh beneficiary in any future pregnancy.
- Benefits can be availed only on the basis of the beneficiary's Aadhaar number (to avoid duplication). See "Documents required" for women who do not yet have Aadhaar.

## Benefits
- First child: ₹5,000 in two instalments.
  - First instalment: ₹3,000. Condition: registration of pregnancy and at least one antenatal check-up (ANC) within 6 months from the LMP (last menstrual period) date, at the Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or an approved health facility identified by the State/UT.
  - Second instalment: ₹2,000. Conditions: (i) the child's birth is registered, and (ii) the child has received the first cycle of BCG, OPV, DPT and Hepatitis-B vaccination, or its equivalent/substitute.
- Second child, if it is a girl: ₹6,000 in one instalment after the birth.
- Additional cash under Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) continues to be available after institutional delivery, so that on average a woman gets ₹6,000 (for the first child, PMMVY plus JSY).
- PW&LM are also entitled to a free meal during pregnancy and for six months after childbirth through the local Anganwadi.

## Documents required
- For registration and the first instalment (Form 1-A):
  - Duly filled Form 1-A.
  - Copy of the MCP card (Mother and Child Protection card).
  - Proof of identity: Aadhaar card, or a permitted alternate ID proof.
  - Bank or Post Office account details (copy of the passbook page showing name, account number and bank name).
  - Mobile number of the beneficiary, her husband or a family member.
- For the second instalment of the first child (Form 1-C):
  - Copy of the child's birth registration.
  - Copy of the MCP card showing the child has received the first cycle of immunisation (or equivalent/substitute).
- Alternate identity proofs accepted in Form 1-A if the woman has no Aadhaar:
  - Bank or Post Office photo passbook
  - Voter ID card
  - Ration card
  - Kisan photo passbook
  - Passport
  - Driving licence
  - PAN card
  - MGNREGS job card
  - Husband's employee photo ID issued by the Government or a Public Sector Undertaking
  - Any other photo ID card issued by the State Government or UT Administration
  - Certificate of identity with photograph issued by a Gazetted Officer on official letterhead
  - Health card issued by a Primary Health Centre (PHC) or Government hospital
  - Any other document specified by the State Government or UT Administration
- A woman without Aadhaar can give her Aadhaar Enrolment ID (EID), or apply for Aadhaar enrolment using Form 2-C, along with a proof of identity in Form 1-A.

## How to apply
1. Register at the Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or approved health facility. Which one depends on whether your State/UT runs PMMVY through the Women and Child Development / Social Welfare Department or the Health Department.
2. Submit Form 1-A, complete in all respects, with the documents listed above and the signed undertaking/consent.
3. Forms are free at the AWC or approved health facility. They can also be downloaded from the Ministry of Women and Child Development website (wcd.nic.in) and submitted at the AWC or approved health facility.
4. For the second instalment, submit Form 1-C after the child's birth is registered and the first cycle of immunisation is done.
5. The Anganwadi Worker, ASHA or ANM helps open an Aadhaar-seeded Bank or Post Office account if the woman does not have one, or helps seed her existing account with Aadhaar.
   - Form 2-A: seed a bank account with Aadhaar.
   - Form 2-B: seed a Post Office account with Aadhaar.
   - Form 2-C: enrol for Aadhaar or update UIDAI details.
6. To change details later (address, mobile number, adding Aadhaar, change of bank/post office account, change of name as in Aadhaar), submit Form 3.
7. Time limit: apply at any time, but not later than 730 days of pregnancy. The LMP date in the MCP card is taken as the date of pregnancy.

## Status check
- The guideline aims for payment into the beneficiary's account preferably within 30 days of registration and submission of a complete claim.
- Payments are approved by:
  - the CDPO (Anganwadi Services), where the scheme runs through the WCD / Social Welfare Department; or
  - the Medical Officer (MO) at block level, where it runs through the Health Department.
- To check a claim, ask the Anganwadi Worker, ASHA or ANM where you registered. Cases are tracked in the PMMVY-CAS software, which is used by officials (not a public status page).
- If money went to a different bank account: in Aadhaar-based payments, money goes to the latest Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the beneficiary (from the PMMVY FAQ booklet).

## Official contacts
- Your local Anganwadi Centre (AWC), ASHA or ANM, and the CDPO or block Medical Officer.
- Forms: Ministry of Women and Child Development website, wcd.nic.in.

## Notes
- Women already registered under PMMVY 1.0: if she had received the first instalment under PMMVY 1.0, she continues under the old three-instalment norms. If she had received the first and second instalments, she receives the remaining benefit under PMMVY 2.0 rules. See the file "PMMVY 1.0 old rules".
- New beneficiaries whose LMP date is after publication of the Mission Shakti guidelines are paid under PMMVY 2.0 rules.
- Beneficiary records are confidential. Aadhaar, bank and other personal details must not be made public. Lists of beneficiaries and entitlements may be displayed at the AWC for transparency.
- The guideline mentions a provision (para 6.47) for women who met the conditions but could not register or claim in time. That paragraph was not found in the supplied document, so the late-claim procedure is unconfirmed.
- The exact form for claiming the ₹6,000 second-girl-child benefit is not named in the supplied text (Form 1-A has a field "Applying for 1st Child / 2nd Child (in case it is a Girl child)"). Unconfirmed: confirm with the AWC.
- Private hospitals: the older PMMVY FAQ booklet says only government hospitals can register or update the MCP card. A woman treated in a private hospital can still claim if the conditions are certified by a government doctor, or by a health department official not below the rank of ANM. Whether this still applies under PMMVY 2.0 is unconfirmed.

## Overview
- JSY is a safe motherhood intervention under the National Health Mission (Ministry of Health & Family Welfare).
- Aim: reduce maternal and neonatal deaths by promoting institutional delivery among poor pregnant women.
- Launched on 12 April 2005. Implemented in all States and UTs, with special focus on Low Performing States (LPS).
- It is a centrally sponsored scheme that combines cash assistance with delivery and post-delivery care.
- ASHAs are the link between the government and pregnant women under JSY.

## Eligibility
- Low Performing States (LPS): Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Bihar, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Rajasthan, Odisha and Jammu & Kashmir. All pregnant women delivering in government health centres (Sub Centre, PHC, CHC, FRU, general wards of district or state hospitals) are eligible.
- High Performing States (HPS, all other states and UTs): BPL, SC and ST women delivering in government health centres are eligible.
- In both LPS and HPS: BPL, SC and ST women delivering in accredited private institutions are eligible.

## Benefits
- Cash assistance for institutional delivery (in ₹):

| Category | Area | Mother's package | ASHA's package | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LPS | Rural | 1,400 | 600 | 2,000 |
| LPS | Urban | 1,000 | 400 | 1,400 |
| HPS | Rural | 700 | 600 | 1,300 |
| HPS | Urban | 600 | 400 | 1,000 |

- The ASHA's package is separate from the mother's money. In rural areas it is ₹300 for the ANC component and ₹300 for facilitating institutional delivery. In urban areas it is ₹200 for ANC and ₹200 for facilitating institutional delivery.
- JSY money is in addition to PMMVY. For the first child, PMMVY plus JSY gives on average ₹6,000 (Mission Shakti guidelines).

## How to apply
1. Register the pregnancy with the ASHA / ANM and get ANC check-ups.
2. Deliver in a government health facility, or an accredited private institution (BPL/SC/ST women only).
3. The JSY cash is processed through the health facility.

## Notes
- Required documents, payment timing, cash assistance for home delivery and for caesarean section were not covered in the source used. These are unconfirmed; ask the ASHA, ANM or the health facility.
- Tamil Nadu is not in the LPS list, so HPS rates and eligibility apply there.

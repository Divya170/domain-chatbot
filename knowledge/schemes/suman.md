## Overview
- SUMAN (Surakshit Matritva Aashwasan) is a Ministry of Health & Family Welfare initiative that turns maternal and newborn entitlements into a service guarantee.
- It promotes safe pregnancy, childbirth and immediate postpartum care with respect and dignity, with no denial of services at public health facilities.
- It brings existing initiatives (such as JSSK, PMSMA and LaQshya) under one umbrella.

## Eligibility
- All pregnant women.
- All mothers up to 6 months after delivery.
- All sick infants.

## Benefits
- Service guarantee at public health facilities:
  - Respectful care that protects privacy and dignity.
  - Free transport from home to the health institution (dial 102 / 108), and free drop back home.
  - At least four antenatal check-ups: one in the first trimester, and at least one under PMSMA in the 2nd or 3rd trimester.
  - Cashless delivery, including caesarean section and management of complications.
  - Free diagnostics, medicines and other consumables.
  - Services by trained personnel, including midwives / skilled birth attendants.
  - Safe motherhood booklet and Mother & Child Protection (MCP) card.
  - Zero-dose / birth-dose vaccination of the newborn.
  - Six home-based newborn care visits.
  - Support for early and continued breastfeeding.
  - Postnatal services and counselling, including family planning.
  - Cashless services for sick neonates and infants.
  - Conditional cash transfers / DBT under central and state schemes (for example JSY, PMMVY).
  - Comprehensive abortion care as per the MTP Act.
  - Time-bound grievance redressal through a call centre, helpline or web portal.

## How to apply
1. No application is needed. Go to any public health facility for ANC, delivery, postnatal or sick-infant care.
2. Call 102 / 108 for free transport.

## Status check
- Complaints about denial of free services, informal payments or disrespectful care can be registered through the grievance helpline (104), call centre or the SUMAN web portal.

## Official contacts
- Free transport: 102 / 108.
- Grievance redressal: 104.
- Client feedback: "Mera Aspatal".

## Notes
- Feedback and grievances are reviewed by the District Quality Assurance Committee (DQAC) and block-level committees.
- Facilities under SUMAN are expected to meet quality standards certified through NQAS / LaQshya.

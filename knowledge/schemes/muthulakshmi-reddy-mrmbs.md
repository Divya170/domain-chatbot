## Overview
- A Tamil Nadu State Government maternity benefit scheme run by the Health and Family Welfare Department for poor pregnant mothers.
- The Tamil Nadu Health and Family Welfare Department has enhanced the financial assistance from ₹12,000 to ₹18,000 (Tamil Nadu district government page).
- The scheme has been integrated with PMMVY 2.0 in Tamil Nadu and is applied for through the PICME portal using the mother's RCH ID (press reports).

## Benefits
- Total assistance: ₹18,000.
- Unconfirmed (from non-official sources): ₹14,000 in cash instalments plus ₹4,000 worth of "Amma Maternity Nutrition Kits". The number and timing of instalments have changed over time (reports mention five, later three, instalments).

## How to apply
1. Register the pregnancy on PICME and get the RCH ID (see the PICME file).
2. Apply through the PICME portal (Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Maternity Benefit Scheme option, enter the mother's RCH ID) or through the Village Health Nurse.

## Official contacts
- Village Health Nurse / Urban Health Nurse, or the nearest Primary Health Centre in Tamil Nadu.

## Notes
- Eligibility (age limit, number of deliveries covered, income criteria), the instalment schedule and the documents required are NOT confirmed from an official guideline. Non-official sources mention a minimum age of 19 years and coverage of the first two deliveries. Treat all such details as unconfirmed and ask the VHN or PHC.
- The launch year is reported inconsistently (1987 or 1989) and is unconfirmed.

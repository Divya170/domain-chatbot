## Overview
- PICME (Pregnancy and Infant Cohort Monitoring and Evaluation) is the Tamil Nadu government's system for registering and tracking every pregnancy and infant.
- Registration gives the mother a 12-digit RCH (Reproductive and Child Health) ID.
- The RCH ID is linked to maternity benefits (for example the Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Maternity Benefit Scheme) and is needed for the child's birth certificate in Tamil Nadu.

## Eligibility
- Pregnant women in Tamil Nadu. The PICME FAQ says all pregnant women should register their pregnancy in PICME.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar details and a pregnancy confirmation document are uploaded during online self-registration (as reported by the Directorate of Public Health and Preventive Medicine through the press).
- The PICME FAQ says Aadhaar is not compulsory for pregnancy registration (details unconfirmed).

## How to apply
1. Online self-registration: open the official PICME portal (picme.tn.gov.in) and choose "Pre-registration" / "Self registration for RCH ID".
2. Enter the required details. A pre-registration ID is generated and sent by SMS to the registered mobile.
3. The Village Health Nurse (VHN) / Urban Health Nurse (UHN) completes the registration, and the RCH ID is issued.
4. Alternatively, register through the VHN / UHN or at the nearest Primary Health Centre.

## Status check
- On the public PICME page (picme.tn.gov.in/picme_public), enter the pre-registration ID or mobile number to download the acknowledgement, check whether the pregnancy is registered and view the RCH ID.

## Official contacts
- Village Health Nurse / Urban Health Nurse, or the nearest Primary Health Centre.

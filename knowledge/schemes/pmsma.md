## Overview
- PMSMA is a Ministry of Health & Family Welfare campaign that provides a fixed-day, quality antenatal check-up (ANC) every month across the country.
- It is held on the 9th day of every month. If the 9th is a Sunday or holiday, it is held on the next working day.
- Aim: every pregnant woman gets at least one check-up by a Medical Officer / OBGY specialist in the 2nd and 3rd trimester of pregnancy.
- PMSMA is in addition to the routine ANC at health facilities. Routine ANC continues as usual.
- It also detects, refers, treats and follows up high-risk pregnancies.

## Eligibility
- All pregnant women in the 2nd and 3rd trimester of pregnancy.
- Special focus on women who have not registered for ANC, women who registered but missed ANC, and high-risk pregnant women.

## Benefits
- Free check-up: all pregnant women are entitled to free ANC check-ups under JSSK.
- Where: identified public health facilities and volunteering accredited private clinics.
  - Rural: PHC, CHC, rural hospital, sub-district hospital, district hospital, medical college hospital.
  - Urban: urban dispensary, urban health post, maternity home.
- Services in the package:
  - History, examination, blood pressure, abdominal examination and fetal heart sound check.
  - Basic tests: haemoglobin, urine albumin and sugar, blood sugar, rapid malaria test, rapid VDRL (syphilis) test, blood grouping; HIV where available.
  - One ultrasound (USG) is recommended for all pregnant women during the 2nd/3rd trimester.
  - Medicines: injection T.T., IFA (iron folic acid) tablets, calcium, and treatment of complications.
  - Counselling (individually or in groups) on care during pregnancy, danger signs, birth preparedness and complication readiness, family planning, nutrition (iron folic acid and calcium), rest, safe sex, institutional delivery, referral transport, JSY and JSSK entitlements, postnatal care, breastfeeding and complementary feeding.
  - Women with unwanted pregnancies are provided safe abortion care services after counselling.
- MCP card colour stickers show the woman's risk status at each visit:
  - Green: no risk factor detected.
  - Red: high-risk pregnancy.
  - Blue: pregnancy-induced hypertension.
  - Yellow: pregnancy with co-morbid conditions such as diabetes, hypothyroidism, STIs.
- High-risk women are referred to higher facilities, where JSSK help desks guide them. Referral can use 108/102, state-owned or empanelled private ambulances.

## How to apply
1. No application is needed. The ASHA / ANM line-lists pregnant women in the 2nd and 3rd trimester by the 5th of every month and mobilises them.
2. Go to the nearest PMSMA site (government facility or volunteering private clinic) on the 9th of the month, with your MCP card.
3. Registration, then lab tests (reports ideally within an hour), then examination by ANM/Staff Nurse and the Medical Officer/OBGY, then counselling and medicines.

## Official contacts
- ASHA, ANM or Anganwadi Worker in your area, or the nearest PHC/CHC.

## Notes
- PMSMA facilities include a feedback and grievance redressal step in the patient flow.
- Private doctors (especially obstetricians/gynaecologists) can volunteer at public facilities. Private facilities can be empanelled to give free PMSMA services on the designated day.

## Overview
- These are the original PMMVY rules (PMMVY 1.0, from 1 January 2017). They were replaced by PMMVY 2.0 under Mission Shakti.
- They still matter for a woman who had already received the first PMMVY 1.0 instalment. She continues under the three-instalment norms.
- For all new registrations, use the PMMVY 2.0 rules in the main PMMVY file.

## Eligibility
- First living child of the family only.
- Pregnancy on or after 1 January 2017.
- Excluded: women in regular employment with Central/State Government or PSUs, or receiving similar benefits under any law.
- Pregnant and lactating AWWs, AWHs and ASHAs could also avail the benefit.

## Benefits
- ₹5,000 in three instalments:
  - First instalment: ₹1,000. Early registration of pregnancy in the MCP card within 150 days from the LMP date.
  - Second instalment: ₹2,000. At least one antenatal check-up. Can be claimed only after 180 days (6 months) from the LMP date.
  - Third instalment: ₹2,000. Child birth registered, and the child has received the first cycle of BCG, OPV, DPT and Hepatitis-B (or equivalent).
- With JSY after institutional delivery, a woman got on average ₹6,000.

## Documents required
- First instalment: Form 1A, copy of MCP card, copy of identity proof, copy of Bank/Post Office passbook.
- Second instalment: Form 1B, copy of MCP card.
- Third instalment: Form 1C, copy of MCP card, copy of Aadhaar ID, copy of child birth registration certificate. Aadhaar was mandatory for the third instalment in all states except J&K, Assam and Meghalaya (Aadhaar of both the beneficiary and her husband).

## Notes
- The LMP date in the MCP card was mandatory for the first and second instalments. Without it, only the third instalment could be claimed.
- Claims had to be made within 730 days from the LMP date, or 460 days from the child's birth if the LMP date was unavailable.
- Claim dates had to be in sequence (first ≤ second ≤ third), but could be entered in any order.
- In case of miscarriage/still birth after an instalment, the remaining instalments could be claimed in a future pregnancy. (PMMVY 2.0 changed this: she is now treated as a fresh beneficiary.)
- In case of infant mortality, she could not claim again if all instalments had already been received.
- A beneficiary who received only the first instalment under the old Maternity Benefit Programme (IGMSY) could claim only the third PMMVY instalment.

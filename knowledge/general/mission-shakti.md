## Overview
- Mission Shakti is an integrated women empowerment programme of the Ministry of Women and Child Development, for the 15th Finance Commission period 2021-22 to 2025-26. Its norms apply from 1 April 2022.
- It has two sub-schemes:
  - **Sambal** (safety and security of women): One Stop Centre (OSC), Women Helpline (181), Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP), and Nari Adalat (women's collectives for alternative dispute resolution).
  - **Samarthya** (empowerment of women): Ujjwala, Swadhar Greh, Working Women Hostel, the creche scheme (Palna) and Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY). It also includes Hubs for Empowerment of Women and gap funding for economic empowerment.

## Benefits
- **One Stop Centre (Sakhi)**: integrated support for women affected by violence, in public or private spaces, under one roof: medical, legal, temporary shelter, police assistance, and psychological counselling. Being set up in every district.
- **Women Helpline 181**: toll-free, 24 hours. Connects women to emergency services (112) and One Stop Centres, and gives information on government schemes. It can also be reached by text for women who cannot speak.
- **Palna (creche)**: day care for children of all mothers irrespective of employment status, with sleeping facilities, early stimulation (under 3 years), pre-school education (3–6 years), supplementary nutrition, growth monitoring, health check-ups and immunisation.
- **PMMVY 2.0**: maternity benefit, see the PMMVY file.

## Notes
- Mahila Shakti Kendra and Mahila Police Volunteers were discontinued under Mission Shakti.

## Overview
- Ways to complain if you are denied a free service, asked to pay at a government hospital, or have not received a scheme benefit.

## How to apply
1. **Free care at a government hospital (JSSK / SUMAN)**:
   - Use the help desk or the suggestion/complaint box at the government health facility.
   - Contact the grievance redressal authority whose name and phone number must be displayed at the facility, district and state level.
   - Call 104 (SUMAN grievance redressal) or register a complaint on the SUMAN web portal.
   - Facilities should keep fixed hours (at least 1 hour on any two working days a week) to hear complaints about free entitlements (JSSK).
2. **PMMVY payment not received**:
   - First ask the Anganwadi Worker / ASHA / ANM where you registered. If the application had an error, it can be corrected through the correction process (Form 3 for changing details).
   - Approving authority: the CDPO (where the WCD/Social Welfare Department runs PMMVY) or the block Medical Officer (where the Health Department runs it).
   - Panchayats take part in addressing grievances and social audits. PMMVY is an agenda point in Gram Sabhas and Mahila Sabhas.
3. **Women in distress (violence, harassment)**:
   - Call 181 (Women Helpline) or go to the One Stop Centre (Sakhi) in your district. For emergencies call 112.

## Notes
- The bot cannot file a complaint on your behalf and cannot see your application.
- Exact time limits for resolving grievances are not stated in the supplied documents (JSSK says "within a suitable timeframe").

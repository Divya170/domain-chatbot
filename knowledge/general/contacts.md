## Overview
- Helpline numbers that appear in the official documents used for this knowledge base.
- In any medical emergency during pregnancy or after delivery, go to the nearest government hospital or call an ambulance immediately.

## Official contacts
- **102 / 108**: free ambulance / transport from home to a health facility, between facilities on referral, and drop back home (SUMAN brochure; PMSMA guideline lists 108/102 for referring high-risk women).
- **104**: grievance redressal under SUMAN (complaints about denial of free services, informal payments or disrespectful care).
- **112**: Emergency Response Support System (ERSS) for police, fire and ambulance (Mission Shakti).
- **181**: Women Helpline. Toll-free, 24 hours. For women in distress and for information on government schemes, legal aid and counselling. Connects to 112 for emergencies and to One Stop Centres (Mission Shakti).
- **1098**: Childline (integrated with the Women Helpline, per Mission Shakti).
- **One Stop Centre (Sakhi)**: one in each district for women affected by violence. Provides medical, legal, police, temporary shelter and counselling support under one roof (Mission Shakti).
- **PMMVY forms**: Ministry of Women and Child Development website, wcd.nic.in.
- **Local help for all schemes**: Anganwadi Worker (AWW), ASHA, ANM, Village/Urban Health Nurse, Primary Health Centre.

## Notes
- The 102/108 ambulance services are run by each State; numbers can differ by State. Confirm locally.
- A State-specific maternal health helpline and the PMMVY national helpline were not found in the supplied documents. They are not included.

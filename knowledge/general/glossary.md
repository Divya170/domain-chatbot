## Overview
- **ANC**: Antenatal care / antenatal check-up, the check-ups during pregnancy.
- **PNC**: Postnatal care, the care after delivery.
- **LMP**: Last Menstrual Period. Its date (recorded in the MCP card) is used to count the stage of pregnancy.
- **MCP card**: Mother and Child Protection card. Records pregnancy check-ups and the child's vaccinations. Needed for PMMVY claims.
- **RCH ID**: Reproductive and Child Health ID, a 12-digit pregnancy registration number (in Tamil Nadu given through PICME).
- **PW&LM**: Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers.
- **AWC / AWW / AWH**: Anganwadi Centre / Anganwadi Worker / Anganwadi Helper.
- **ASHA**: Accredited Social Health Activist, the village-level health worker.
- **ANM**: Auxiliary Nurse Midwife.
- **VHN / UHN**: Village Health Nurse / Urban Health Nurse (Tamil Nadu).
- **PHC / CHC / FRU / DH**: Primary Health Centre / Community Health Centre / First Referral Unit / District Hospital.
- **CDPO**: Child Development Project Officer, the approving authority for PMMVY where it is run by the Women and Child Development Department.
- **DBT**: Direct Benefit Transfer, money paid directly into the beneficiary's bank or post office account.
- **Aadhaar-seeded account**: a bank or post office account linked to the account holder's Aadhaar number.
- **BPL**: Below Poverty Line. **SC / ST**: Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes.
- **LPS / HPS**: Low Performing States / High Performing States (used in JSY).
- **Institutional delivery**: childbirth in a health facility instead of at home.
- **IFA**: Iron Folic Acid tablets. **TT**: Tetanus Toxoid injection.
- **USG**: Ultrasonography (ultrasound scan).
- **High-risk pregnancy**: a pregnancy with a risk factor needing special care and referral (marked with a red sticker on the MCP card under PMSMA).
- **BCG, OPV, DPT, Hepatitis-B**: the first cycle of the baby's vaccinations, needed for the last PMMVY instalment.
- **NHM**: National Health Mission (earlier NRHM, National Rural Health Mission).
- **OSC**: One Stop Centre (Sakhi) for women affected by violence.
- **ERSS**: Emergency Response Support System (112).
